MEMORANDUM

DATE: 15 August 2026

SUBJECT: Founding of a High-Performance Bitcoin SHA-256 Engineering Operation


PURPOSE

Establish a specialist software and systems-engineering company dedicated to developing high-quality, high-performance Bitcoin mining software designed to improve the efficiency, reliability and economic competitiveness of SHA-256 mining operations.


CORE PROPOSITION

The company should build a vertically integrated SHA-256 performance stack:

        ASIC HARDWARE
             │
             ▼
      LOW-LEVEL FIRMWARE
             │
             ▼
       SHA-256 ENGINE
             │
             ▼
     WORK DISPATCH / STRATUM
             │
             ▼
       TELEMETRY ENGINE
             │
             ▼
   PERFORMANCE OPTIMISATION
             │
             ▼
       MINING ECONOMICS


TECHNICAL PROGRAMME

Build the core infrastructure primarily in RUST, concentrating on:

• ASIC communication and work scheduling
• Stratum infrastructure
• High-performance networking
• Telemetry
• Fault detection
• Thermal and power monitoring
• Firmware interfaces
• Latency reduction
• Automatic recovery
• Fleet orchestration
• Performance benchmarking

Develop a complementary R quantitative research environment for:

• Hashrate analysis
• J/TH optimisation
• Energy economics
• Monte Carlo modelling
• ASIC benchmarking
• Profitability analysis
• Firmware A/B testing
• Fleet-level optimisation


COMPETITIVE PHILOSOPHY

The operation should compete through engineering quality rather than merely
purchasing more machines.

The central research question is:

"How much additional economically useful SHA-256 work can superior software
extract from the same hardware and electricity supply?"

The objective is therefore to optimise:

• Effective hashrate
• Energy efficiency
• Uptime
• Share acceptance
• Hardware utilisation
• Thermal stability
• Power efficiency
• Cost per unit of useful hashing


INITIAL DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMME

Phase I — Research

Establish a controlled laboratory environment for benchmarking ASICs,
networking, firmware behaviour, power consumption and thermal performance.

Phase II — Software

Develop the Rust-based mining control and orchestration layer together with
the R-based quantitative research and optimisation environment.

Phase III — Benchmarking

Compare the system against established mining software using controlled,
repeatable measurements.

Phase IV — Production

Deploy progressively to a small production fleet, measure real-world
performance, and use the resulting telemetry to improve the software.

Phase V — Scaling

Expand the software across larger fleets and develop commercial software,
management and optimisation products for Bitcoin mining operators.


OPERATING PRINCIPLE

The company should not define success simply as maximum nominal hashrate.

The relevant metric is:

ECONOMICALLY USEFUL SHA-256 OUTPUT
=
HASHRATE × UPTIME × ACCEPTANCE × ENERGY EFFICIENCY

subject to:

• Hardware limitations
• Electricity cost
• Thermal constraints
• Network conditions
• Reliability
• Capital expenditure
• Operating expenditure


LONG-TERM OBJECTIVE

Build a reference-quality, auditable and highly optimised Bitcoin mining
software stack capable of extracting the maximum economically useful
performance from available SHA-256 hardware.

The long-term ambition is not simply to become another Bitcoin mining
operator.

It is to become a specialist computational-engineering company whose
principal competitive advantage is superior SHA-256 software, control
systems, data analysis and hardware utilisation.


STRATEGIC THESIS

"In an ASIC-dominated Bitcoin market, the next competitive frontier is not
necessarily more hardware — it is better software, better control systems,
better data and better utilisation of every joule."
